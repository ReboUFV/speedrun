How "Pareto" is this list?

In German, the ~1,000 most frequent words cover roughly 75–80% of everyday text, but a big share of that comes from small function words (der, und, ist, nicht…) that you already know. This list is the content-word part of that core: – nouns, adjectives and adverbs. Learn these plus the verbs from your grammar units and you have the backbone of B1–B2 texts.
