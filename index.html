Routines

📅Weekends Spaced output days
Morning · Synthetic commute

Force a 40 min walk. Manufacture a commute; walk purely to run your Michel Thomas / Language Transfer active audio block.

Mid-day · Mega-block (3.5 h)

Phase 1: clear all Anki reviews.
Phase 2: advance the day's units in the plan.
Phase 3: run 1 AI C-Test.

C-Test prompt
Act as a B1/B2 German exam generator. Create a short, 75-word C-Test (Lückentext) paragraph about [Topic]. CRITICAL RULE: Use only A2/B1 vocabulary. Remove the second half of every second word. Wait for my answers, then grade my basic grammar.
Afternoon · Life admin

Passive echo: while doing chores or gym, run the same audio you studied on your morning walk.

Night · Essay & output (1 h)

Saturday: 1 full 150-word essay, AI-graded, logged in Essays. Then deep immersion (games/shows).
Sunday: 1 essay, graded, logged. Open Audit for the weekly review.

The day's units from the plan. Type every answer.

Priority 2 · Reality check

C-Test grind: let AI generate a Lückentext to expose missing endings.

C-Test prompt
Act as a B1/B2 German exam generator. Create a short, 75-word C-Test (Lückentext) paragraph about [Topic]. CRITICAL RULE: Use only A2/B1 vocabulary. Remove the second half of every second word. Wait for my answers, then grade my basic grammar.
Priority 2 · Output

AI interrogation (20 min): text chat in German.

Interrogation prompt
Act as a strict German Syntax Drill Master. Ask me one B2-level question at a time about [Insert Topic]. Wait for my answer. If I make a mistake with the V2 rule, Verb-Kickers (dass/weil), or cases (Acc/Dat), give me a brutal 1-sentence correction. Then ask the next question.
🌙Evenings
Short evening

15 min audio-drill chat, then 1 episode of a German series. Sleep.

Audio-drill prompt
Act as a strict German Drill Master. Give me sentences from the first 3 hours of Michel Thomas or first 17 lessons of Language Transfer in English. Wait for my German translation. Correct my V2/Modal grammar brutally, then give the next sentence.
Long evening (3–5 h)

Hour 1: write a 150-word text, let AI grade it.
Then: deep immersion. Close the books; games / series in German.

Essay grader prompt
Act as a strict CEFR B2 German examiner. Grade my 150-word text. Give me a score based on B2 criteria. Highlight every grammar/case error, explain it briefly, and rewrite the sentence so it sounds natural and uses B2 connective structures (um...zu, weil, obwohl).
