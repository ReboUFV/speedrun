Cards saved while Anki is closed, or on your phone, wait here. Sync them at your computer or export a CSV.

Connect to Anki desktop, once

  1. In Anki desktop open Tools, Add-ons, Get Add-ons, paste 2055492159 (AnkiConnect) and restart Anki.
  2. Open Tools, Add-ons, AnkiConnect, Config and add this site to webCorsOriginList:
"webCorsOriginList": [
    "http://localhost",
    "https://reboufv.github.io"
]
  1. Restart Anki and keep it open. If the browser asks to access devices on your local network, allow it.
  2. The status at the top turns green.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on the computer that runs Anki; Safari blocks it. On your phone, save to the queue and export a CSV, then import it in Anki with "Allow HTML" on.
