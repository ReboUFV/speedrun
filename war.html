10:30 Ladder drill
  1. 30 items from A2 to C1, timed: 20 minutes, no notes.
  2. Score per level. Your ceiling is the first level under 70%.
  3. Every mistake at or below your ceiling goes on the careless list (a note on your phone): my answer → correct → rule.
Ladder drill, A2 to C1
Act as the grammar section of a university German placement exam that places students from A1 to C1. Give me 30 numbered items at once, easiest first: 8 at A2, 8 at B1, 8 at B2 and 6 at C1, multiple choice with 3 or 4 options and one-word gap fills. Don't show the levels. Focus on: verb position, cases after prepositions and verbs, adjective endings, verbs with prepositions, connectors (als/wenn, obwohl/trotzdem, damit/um … zu), passive, Konjunktiv II and relative pronouns, plus these weak spots of mine: [from the mini test]. No answers yet. When I send mine: score per level, my estimated placement (highest level at 70%+), then each mistake as my answer → correct → rule in 5 words. Mark the mistakes at or below my placement as careless.

12:00 Listening loop
  1. Material at B1, not B2 (Nicos Weg B1, DW Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten, Goethe B1 model test). If you catch only loose words, it's too hard: go down.
  2. Clips of 30–60 seconds. 1: listen blind and note what you got. 2: listen reading the transcript. 3: stop at each sentence you missed: unknown word, known word you didn't recognise, or speed? 4: listen blind again. 5: shadow it 2–3 times.
  3. Start at 0.75× if needed, always finish at 1×.
  4. Chunks you didn't recognise go to New words in Anki, with the audio sentence.

14:30 Reading
  1. Two texts: one at your level, one a level above.
  2. Questions first (2 minutes), underline the key word in each. Then find the paragraph; read only that.
  3. Paraphrase hunt: the right option says the same with other words; an option that copies the text's words is often the trap.
  4. Post-mortem for every miss: quote the sentence that held the answer and name the cause (word, paraphrase, trap, time).
Exam-style reading at my level

Then 7 questions: 4 richtig / falsch / nicht im Text and 3 multiple choice with 3 options. Like real exams, put paraphrases in the correct options and word-matching traps or absolute words (immer, alle, nie) in the wrong ones. Wait for my answers. Then grade them; for each mistake, quote the sentence that holds the answer and name the trap.

16:45 Listening, exam format
  1. One part of a real model test (Goethe B1, then B2 from 2 Nov), played as in the exam: once or twice as the instructions say, no pausing.
  2. Use the reading time: underline key words, predict each answer.
  3. Listen for turns: aber, eigentlich, doch, nicht … sondern. Watch numbers and negation.
  4. Score it, then do the loop from 12:00 on the parts you missed.
